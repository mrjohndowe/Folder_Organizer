PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Folder Organizer is designed to organize files on your Windows computer. Your files stay on your computer unless you choose to move, share, or upload them yourself.

Information the application uses

To organize files, Folder Organizer reads file and folder names, locations, file types, and basic file dates from the folders you select. It uses this information only to show you a preview, apply the organization rules you choose, and keep a local history of completed moves.

Local storage

The application stores its settings, custom rules, ignored items, version information, and organization history in a local SQLite database on your device. This information is not sent to Folder Organizer or to its developer.

Updates

When update checking is enabled, Folder Organizer contacts GitHub to check whether a newer release is available and to download an installer only when you approve the update. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

Website

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The site does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers, accounts, or contact forms. GitHub may collect technical information as part of providing its hosting service.

Your choices

You decide which folders to scan and which proposed moves to apply. Folder Organizer does not permanently delete files automatically. You can remove the application's local data by uninstalling the app and deleting its local data folder.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact mrjohndowe@outlook.com.
